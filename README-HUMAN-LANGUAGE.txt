SPEAKUP 120 — FINAL HUMAN-LANGUAGE VERSION

Core design principle:
The goal is natural human communication, not AI-sounding or textbook English.

The program now explicitly teaches:
- Natural human wording
- Professional but conversational workplace English
- When to be casual vs professional
- Concise language instead of unnecessary formality
- Spoken phrases real people use
- Human-language checks before sending or saying something
- Distinguishing grammatically correct language from language that actually sounds natural

Examples:
AI/textbook:
“I am writing to follow up regarding the matter referenced below.”

Natural:
“Just following up on the email below.”

Important:
The site is still a standalone browser website. Progress and notes remain local to the browser.
